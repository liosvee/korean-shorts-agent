# Find and inspect relevant Shorts

1. Run `python -m ko_shorts discover PROJECT --days 90 --pages 2` for the selected
   keyword. Use --query for adjacent Korean terms as needed. Each discovery
   creates a separate dated snapshot. Merge evidence carefully; deduplicate IDs.
2. Inspect up to 10 high-performing candidates via permitted playback/browser
   tools. Confirm Shorts format and Korean speech/content; region/language
   search settings alone are insufficient. Translate titles into English.
3. After actual inspection run verify with a concrete evidence note and English
   title. Record spoken opening, scene timestamps, premise, structure, payoff
   and publicly observable audience response in reference-analysis.md. Facts
   about content require viewing or a reliable transcript; metadata is not enough.
4. Use shortlist to compare total views and lifetime average views/day. Neither
   is real-time velocity. Smaller-channel outperformance requires a separately
   collected, comparable baseline; do not invent one. No private retention data.
5. Identify reusable topic or storytelling mechanisms as hypotheses, not causal
   proof. Describe a genuinely distinct angle and new examples for our Short.
   Avoid copying signature wording, scenes or sequencing. Do not download the
   references for reuse. Preserve URLs for attribution in the research report.
6. Continue to prompts/04-script.md. If playback is unavailable, ask for a
   transcript/observations and keep any interim analysis labelled metadata-only.
